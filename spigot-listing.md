## توضیحات — BBCode

```
[CENTER][IMG]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mamad-ta/TA_BlockTrigger/main/assets/logo-512.png[/IMG]

[SIZE=6][B]TA_BlockTrigger[/B][/SIZE]
[SIZE=4][COLOR=#4A9EFF]Turn any block into a command.[/COLOR][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Paper · Spigot · CraftBukkit — 1.20.1+[/SIZE][/CENTER]

[HR][/HR]

[B]Every block can be a button.[/B]

Look at a block, run one command, and that block now does something:

[CODE]/bt console give {player} diamond 1 both[/CODE]

From that moment, anyone who clicks it gets a diamond — paid from the console, so no
permissions needed. Bind [B]/spawn[/B] to a nether portal. Make a shop chest that charges
players. Turn your lobby floor into a teleport pad.

Bindings are tied to [B]one exact block[/B] (world + X/Y/Z), so two identical stone blocks
can do completely different things — and unconfigured blocks are never touched.

[HR][/HR]

[B][SIZE=5]Why you'll like it[/SIZE][/B]

[LIST]
[*][B]Two ways to run[/B] — as the console (with full power) or as the player (normal permissions)
[*][B]Right, left, or both[/B] — pick which click fires
[*][B]Live placeholders[/B] — {player} · {uuid} · {world} · {x} {y} {z}
[*][B]Cooldowns[/B] — stop players spamming your command
[*][B]Block protection[/B] — configured blocks can't be broken by accident
[*][B]Sounds + messages[/B] — 7 sound slots, full colour and hex control, all configurable
[*][B]Live reload[/B] — /bt reload, no restart
[*][B]Zero dependencies[/B] — no NMS, no lag, one 73 KB jar
[/LIST]

[HR][/HR]

[B][SIZE=5]Commands[/SIZE][/B]

[CODE]
/bt <console|player> <command ...> [right|left|both]
/bt remove          /bt reload          /bt help
[/CODE]

Tab completion included. Managed by operators by default.

[HR][/HR]

[B][SIZE=5]Setup takes 20 seconds[/SIZE][/B]

[LIST=1]
[*]Drop the jar in [B]plugins/[/B]
[*]Restart
[*]Look at a block and run [B]/bt[/B] — done
[/LIST]

[HR][/HR]

[CENTER][B][SIZE=4][COLOR=#4A9EFF]Compatible with Paper, Spigot and CraftBukkit · Minecraft 1.20.1 → 1.21.x · Java 17+[/COLOR][/SIZE][/B]

[URL=https://github.com/mamad-ta/TA_BlockTrigger]Source[/URL] · [URL=https://github.com/mamad-ta/TA_BlockTrigger/blob/main/docs/GUIDE.md]Guide[/URL] · [URL=https://github.com/mamad-ta/TA_BlockTrigger/issues]Issues[/URL]

[SIZE=2]If this helps your server, a ⭐ on the repo means a lot.[/SIZE][/CENTER]
```
